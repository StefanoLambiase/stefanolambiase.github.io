My mission is to improve the quality of life for video game developers and designers, enabling them to create products that meaningfully enhance player experiences; it is my way of giving back to them whose work has shaped and inspired me.

I truly enjoy teaching and conducting research that can make a positive impact on society—hopefully in the not-too-distant future. I am always interested in collaborating on research projects and open to new opportunities, so feel free to reach out!

Identifying Critical Success Factors in Video Game Development

A Meta-Analysis Approach (Mitacs grant IT43497)

Research project investigating the critical success factors that shape outcomes in video game development through a meta-analysis of empirical studies. Awarded under the Mitacs program in Canada, with the research visit hosted at Dalhousie University in Halifax.

My role: I wrote and was awarded the proposal. The project is supervised by Prof. Paul Ralph (Dalhousie University) and Prof. Fabio Palomba (University of Salerno).

Academic Projects

Research projects I have contributed to, mostly through co-authored publications and, where noted, project-related reviewing activity.

Human-centered AI integration in Software Engineering

PhD project at Aalborg University, Department of Computer Science

Investigates how AI—particularly large language models and generative tools—can be effectively and responsibly integrated into Software Engineering through a human-centered perspective. Beyond the technical capabilities of AI tools, the project examines the human, social, and organisational dimensions of AI adoption: developers' well-being and skills, team collaboration and human–AI teaming, and organisational culture and policy. It uses a mixed-methods approach combining surveys, interviews, and longitudinal studies.

My role: I co-author publications stemming from this project.

Student Projects

Erwin Bot

🤖 Chat bot for management support built with Azure Cloud Services. Produced for the Cloud Computing course of Computer Science at University of Salerno. It offers the manager the opportunity to communicate in an organized manner with the project team and, at the same time, monitor and organize the various activities under development.

BiblioNet

📕 🎓 Spring Boot Web App for library support. Produced for the Software Engineering and Software Project Management courses of Computer Science at University of Salerno.

DARTS

DARTS (Detection And Refactoring of Test Smells) is an Intellij plug-in which (1) implements a state-of-the-art detection mechanism to detect instances of three test smell types, i.e., General Fixture, Eager Test, and Lack of Cohesion of Test Methods, at commit-level and (2) enables their automated refactoring through the integrated APIs provided by Intellij.
